# Nagrik-Setu

## About Nagrik-Setu
Nagrik-Setu is a digital platform that connects citizens with local authorities to make reporting and resolving civic issues easier and more transparent.
In our daily lives, we come across many problems around us, such as potholes, garbage that has not been collected, broken streetlights, drainage problems, and many other issues that affect the people living in that area. Reporting these problems is important, but the process should not end just after submitting a complaint.
Nagrik-Setu is built around this idea. A citizen can report a civic issue and keep track of what happens after the report is submitted. On the other side, authorities can view these issues, understand their priority, assign them to the concerned department, and keep track of the progress.
The main idea is to keep both sides connected throughout the process. A citizen should be able to see what is happening with an issue they reported, while the authority should have a clear view of the problems that need attention in their area.
Nagrik-Setu is not just about reporting a complaint. It is about keeping the whole process connected, from the time an issue is reported to the time it is acknowledged, assigned, worked on, resolved, and finally verified by the citizen.

## Why Nagrik-Setu?
A civic issue may be easy to notice, but following up on it can often be difficult.
For example, a person may notice a pothole, an overflowing drain, or a broken streetlight and report it to the concerned authority. But after reporting it, the person may not know whether the complaint was seen, who is responsible for it, whether any work has started, or whether the issue has actually been fixed.
The same problem can also be difficult from the authority's side. There can be many complaints at the same time, each with a different level of urgency and sometimes a different department responsible for handling it. Keeping track of all these issues and their progress can become difficult without a proper system.
Nagrik-Setu is meant to bring this entire process together in one place.
For citizens, it provides a way to report issues and stay updated about their progress. For authorities, it provides a way to manage reported issues, prioritize them, assign them to the right departments, and keep track of their resolution.
Another important part of the idea is what happens after an issue is marked as resolved. Simply marking something as "resolved" does not always mean that the problem has actually been solved from the citizen's point of view. Nagrik-Setu therefore includes the idea of showing proof of the work and allowing the citizen to verify the result.
The basic flow is:
**Report → Acknowledge → Assign → Work on the issue → Resolve → Verify**
The goal of Nagrik-Setu is to make this process easier to follow for citizens and easier to manage for authorities.
