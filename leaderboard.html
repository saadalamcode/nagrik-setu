How points work

Points reward constructive civic participation — not activity for its own sake.

Report a verified issue +40 pts
Support a nearby report +5 pts
Verify a resolution +20 pts
Provide additional update +10 pts
Report is resolved +15 pts
Points are not transferable and hold no monetary value. This leaderboard is designed to recognise genuine community contribution.
